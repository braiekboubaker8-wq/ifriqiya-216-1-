IFRIQIYA 216.

Born in Tunisia. Inspired by Ifriqiya. Made for the new generation.

Ifriqiya 216 is more than a clothing brand — it’s a way of expressing where we come from and who we are.

Inspired by Tunisia’s culture, history, streets, and modern youth, we create pieces that bring heritage and contemporary streetwear together.

The name Ifriqiya represents the roots of our land, while 216 represents the country we call home.

Our goal is simple: create clothing with meaning, identity, and attitude.

This is our culture. This is our generation. This is Ifriqiya 216.
